Privacy Policy – Hermes Personal

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Hermes Personal is a personal-use app used only by its owner (clout028@gmail.com). It is not available to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs, Contacts) solely to generate personal summaries and reminders for the owner.

Data use and sharing

  • Data is processed only on the owner's own computer and the AI services the owner has chosen to use, for the owner's benefit.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than providing the owner's personal summaries.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

Short-lived summaries are kept locally and pruned automatically. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
